How Commyute works

The whole thing takes about two minutes to set up.

1

Join with your school email

Sign up with your @bishopodowd.org address. You'll get a verification link — click it and your account goes live. This is the only thing standing between the app and the general public, so it isn't optional.

2

Say whether you drive

Drivers list how many seats they have. Riders just say which mornings they need. If you sometimes do both, pick both.

3

Drop a rough pin

Type your address so the map knows your area. Commyute shifts the pin a block or two before anyone sees it, so people know your neighborhood without knowing your house.

4

Browse who's close

You'll see students sorted by how well they fit — distance, matching days, and whether they drive or ride. Skip anyone you want.

5

Send a request

Write a short note. They'll see your profile and your message, and can accept or pass.

6

Work out the details

Once someone accepts, a chat opens. Agree on a pickup spot and time. When you're both ready, swap numbers and keep it going from there.

Safety and what Commyute does not do

Being straight with you about where the app helps and where it stops.

Commyute introduces students to each other. It does not screen drivers, check insurance, or supervise rides. Everything that happens in the car is between you, the other student, and your families.

Your address stays private

Commyute geocodes what you type, shifts the point by one to two blocks in a random direction, then throws the original away. Nobody — including the admin — can look up where you actually live.

✓

Messages need consent

Chat only opens after you accept a request. Declining is silent.

✓

New drivers can't offer rides

California restricts provisional drivers from carrying passengers under 20 for their first 12 months. Commyute asks for your license date and won't let you post as a driver until you're past that mark.

✓

Block and report

One tap removes someone from your view entirely. Reports go straight to the admin.

Talk to a parent first

Before you ride with someone or drive them, tell a parent or guardian who it is and what the plan is. Commyute is a way to find people, not a substitute for that conversation.

It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or operated by Bishop O'Dowd High School or the Diocese of Oakland, and neither institution controls it or is responsible for anything that happens through it.

3. No screening. Commyute confirms only that an account uses a valid @bishopodowd.org email. It does not run background checks and does not verify licenses, driving records, insurance, vehicle condition, or anyone's character or intentions. Deciding who to ride with, or who to drive, is entirely your call.

4. California provisional license rules. California restricts provisional drivers from carrying passengers under 20 during their first 12 months unless accompanied by a parent, guardian, or licensed driver 25 or older. Commyute asks for your license date and blocks driving features until 12 months have passed, but this relies on what you enter. You are responsible for knowing and following the law regardless of what the app allows.

5. Risk. Riding in or driving a car carries real risk of injury, death, and property damage. By using Commyute to arrange a ride you accept that risk yourself.
